Rules for evidence

  • A result qualifies only the revision and the profile it ran on. Old passing results are never reused as new evidence.
  • Internal and agent-assisted reviews are labeled internal and are never described as independent audits.
  • An original report's verdict is never edited after a fix. The fix is recorded next to the report.
  • Skipped, blocked or timed-out checks count as open gates, not as passes.

The 70 user stories

Each story runs real binaries over RPC, most on a fresh disposable network. When run with the story-test daemon, every finalized block is also checked for supply, delegation-share, nonce and unbonding invariants. The stories that stress restarts and faults (52, 59, 69 and 70) end with a signing-safety assertion that fails on any signing-guard failure.

Load qualification

Run on 2026-09-20 with four validators on one 10-vCPU host and a mixed load of 80% transfers and 20% storage-writing contract calls. Other tenants saturated the host for the whole campaign, so these results are lower bounds.

Offered loadDurationOutcome
20 TPS30 minStable. 35,984 of 35,984 included; inclusion p50 1.5 s, p95 3.8 s
50 TPS60 sStable. Inclusion p50 1.5 s, p95 4.3 s
50 TPS32 minUnstable. Healthy for about 5 minutes, then commit gaps of 42 s to over 133 s; longest block gap 576 s
75 TPS45 sDegraded. 31% included
100 to 200 TPSshortFinality halts after about 600 to 800 inclusions
500 TPS30 sNo blocks produced

At every saturation point the first bottleneck was consensus liveness, not execution, RPC, the mempool or disk. The gas ceiling, about 300 simple transfers per second at 30 million gas per 2 s slot, was never reached.

Re-measurement pending

A fix that lets delayed rounds catch up from authenticated peer progress merged on 2026-10-08, after this campaign. Until load is measured again on uncontended hardware, the planning figure is 20 TPS sustained.

EVM gas-to-time calibration

On 2026-09-26 an adversarial corpus searched for the slowest block per unit of gas. The worst family projected to about 0.76 s for 30 million gas on the campaign host (best of three runs at 29.6 million gas; observed 0.75 to 0.92 s). ECRecover was the outlier and was repriced to 9,000 gas (decision). The MLOAD family of memory-access opcodes, at about 25 ns per gas, are now the binding constraint, to be confirmed on reference validator hardware.
